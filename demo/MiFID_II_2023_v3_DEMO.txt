Paragraph 1. Scope and Objective
The Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (MiFID II) establishes a harmonised regulatory framework for investment services across the European Union. Its primary objective is to increase transparency, improve investor protection and ensure the integrity of EU financial markets.

Paragraph 2. Mandatory Trade Reporting Fields
All investment firms shall report, for each trade, the following fields without omission: Trade_ID, Asset_Class, Execution_Timestamp, Counterparty_LEI, Price, Quantity, Currency, and, where applicable, Execution_Venue. The data must be transmitted to the relevant national competent authority (NCA) within the time limits stipulated in Article 25.

Paragraph 3. Counterparty LEI Requirement
The Counterparty_LEI must be a valid 20-character alphanumeric identifier issued by an accredited Local Operating Unit (LOU) in accordance with the Global Legal Entity Identifier System (GLEIF). A LEI that fails the GLEIF checksum test shall be rejected by the reporting system.

Paragraph 4. Derivative-Specific Obligations
For derivative contracts (including options, futures, swaps and structured products), the Maturity_Date field is mandatory and must be a valid ISO-8601 date (YYYY-MM-DD). In addition, the Underlying_ISIN is required for all derivatives that are not cash-settled.

Paragraph 5. Timing of Reporting
Transactions executed on or after 1 January 2023 shall be reported on a real-time basis (within 10 minutes of execution). Trades executed before that date may be reported on a delayed (end-of-day) basis, but must be flagged as “historical”.

Paragraph 6. Data Quality and Validation
Each reporting submission shall undergo automated validation against the MiFID II reporting schema. Errors identified at validation time must be corrected and re-submitted within 24 hours, otherwise the reporting firm will be subject to supervisory penalties.

Paragraph 7. New Clause – Execution Venue Disclosure (v2.0)
Effective 1 July 2023, all trades executed on multilateral trading facilities (MTFs) or systematic internalisers (SIs) must include the field `Execution_Venue` indicating the market identifier code (MIC) of the venue. Failure to provide a valid MIC will result in a breach notice under Article 28.

Paragraph 8. Synthetic Demo Clause - Reporting Contact (v3.0)
